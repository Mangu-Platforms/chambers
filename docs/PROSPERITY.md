# Chambers Studio monetization (Phase 5)

- **Free:** 1 document, all templates, all exports. Optional "Made with Chambers" line on free PDF
  exports — default off in demo mode.
- **Paid:** $12/mo (Studio) — unlimited documents, share links, version history. Alternative under
  test: $9 one-time export pack for single-use job seekers.
- **Not year-one:** job boards, recruiter marketplaces, team seats. The sheet must win first.

## Kill criteria (product)

- PDF export broken or drifting from the preview → stop selling until fixed. The parity is the product.
- UI indistinguishable from a generic shadcn kit → redesign before any growth spend.
